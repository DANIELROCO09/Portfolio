Project Overview

This project explores the relationship between cryptocurrency market activity and external social-media-adjacent attention signals using structured datasets and public APIs.

The dataset combines historical crypto market information with developer metrics, sentiment indicators, community engagement statistics, and global attention proxies such as Wikimedia page views.

The objective is to analyze whether social attention and behavioral indicators can help explain or anticipate changes in market dynamics.

Engineering Process

Situation

Cryptocurrency markets are heavily influenced by public sentiment, speculative behavior, and social attention, making traditional price-only analysis insufficient.

Task

Build a structured analytical workflow capable of integrating market data with external sentiment and social engagement indicators for exploratory quantitative analysis.

Action

Processed and explored datasets from CoinGecko APIs, Fear & Greed sentiment indexes, and Wikimedia pageview data using Python-based data analysis tools and reproducible data engineering workflows.

Result

Developed a consolidated research-oriented dataset useful for experimentation in financial modeling, behavioral analytics, and sentiment-driven market analysis.
